Guidance

Sponsorship disclosure

How to label sponsored posts so your audience always knows. This is general guidance, not legal advice. Rules differ by country.

  • Label every sponsored post. That includes cash deals, free products, event support and affiliate links.
  • Use the platform's paid partnership label when there is one, such as on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.
  • Put the label where people see it without tapping more. Start of the caption is best.
  • In videos, say it out loud and show it on screen, not only in the caption.
  • Use plain words: Ad, Sponsored, or Paid partnership with the business name. Tags like #sp, #collab or #thanks on their own are not enough.
  • Only share honest opinions about products you have actually used.

In the US, the FTC's Endorsement Guides apply. Read the FTC's Disclosures 101.

Rule

Who can join

Common Pace is for adults only at launch.

  • You must be 18 or older to create an athlete or business account.
  • We ask for your date of birth at sign-up. It is never shown on your profile.
  • Report any account that seems to belong to someone under 18. We review it and remove it if needed.
Rule

How we use AI

AI helps suggest matches and draft text. It never makes decisions for you.

  • AI is off unless you turn it on, at sign-up or in Account settings.
  • When it is on, we send your profile or campaign details to an AI provider. Never your messages, password or payment details.
  • Fit estimates and drafts are labeled AI estimate. Nothing AI writes is published or agreed until you approve it.
  • We do not use Strava data for AI or show it to other users.
Rule

Content rules and reporting

Keep it honest, respectful and legal.

  • No harassment, hate, scams, fake stats or content you don't own.
  • We filter scam requests in messages, such as asks for gift cards, wire transfers, crypto or fees.
  • Report any profile, video, campaign, message or deal. We review reports within [24 hours] and tell you the outcome.
  • You can block any account. Blocked accounts can't message you or see your contact details.
